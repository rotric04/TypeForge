## Overview

TypeForge AI (https://typeforge.fun) is a free AI-powered typing training platform that uses machine learning to help users improve their typing speed (WPM) and accuracy. Unlike basic typing test sites, TypeForge is an adaptive training system that personalizes exercises based on each user's unique typing behavior.

---

## Mission Statement

"Every keystroke makes you better."

TypeForge AI's mission is to help anyone — developers, students, writers, data entry professionals, and competitive typists — build faster, more accurate, and more confident typing habits through AI-driven personalization and deep behavioral analysis.

## Product Features (Detailed)

### 1. Adaptive AI Engine
- Backend ML pipeline: K-Means clustering for user segmentation, XGBoost for error prediction, LightGBM for recommendation generation
- Analyzes every keystroke in real-time: timing, delays, error rate, recovery speed
- Generates training content personalized to each user's current weak spots
- No two users receive the same training sequence

### 2. Character-Level Tracking
- Captures per-character reaction time
- Measures inter-key delay patterns (bigrams, trigrams)
- Tracks backspace frequency and error recovery behavior
- Identifies "problem characters" — letters, numbers, symbols you consistently miss or slow down on

### 3. Typing DNA System
- Unique user profile generated from session data
- Four core scores:
  - **Focus Score** — concentration and consistency during a session
  - **Consistency Score** — variance in speed and error distribution
  - **Confidence Score** — willingness to commit to keystrokes without hesitation
  - **Improvement Rate** — trajectory of growth over time
- Generates a "Typing Archetype" personality label
- Updates every 10 minutes of active training

### 4. Developer Mode
- Code snippet training in 10+ programming languages:
  - Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Rust, Go, SQL, Bash, JSON, HTML, CSS
- Syntax-aware drills (brackets, operators, indentation)
- Detects weak programming-specific characters (brackets, colons, underscores)
- Tailored for software developers, data scientists, and engineers

### 5. Achievement Engine
- 50+ unique badges across multiple categories:
  - Speed badges (Ghost Fingers 100 WPM, Speed Demon 120 WPM)
  - Coding badges (Code Assassin — 100 coding sessions)
  - Consistency badges (Night Owl — 30 midnight sessions)
  - Accuracy badges (Punctuation Slayer — 98% punctuation accuracy)
- XP system with level progression
- Daily quests and weekly challenge system
- Monthly Evolution Reports showing long-term progress

### 6. Analytics Dashboard
- WPM trend charts over time
- Error frequency heatmaps
- Finger fatigue analysis
- Weak bigram detection (letter pairs you slow down on)
- Session heatmaps
- AI-powered improvement forecasts

## Frequently Asked Questions (Extended)

**Q: What is TypeForge AI?**
A: TypeForge AI is a free AI-powered typing training platform at typeforge.fun. It uses machine learning to personalize typing exercises based on your unique keystroke behavior, helping you improve your WPM and accuracy faster than traditional typing tests.

**Q: How is TypeForge different from Monkeytype?**
A: Monkeytype is an excellent speed tester. TypeForge is a full training system. TypeForge analyzes your keystrokes at the character level, assigns you a Typing DNA profile, and uses ML algorithms to generate exercises specifically targeting your weak points. It's closer to having a personal typing coach than a speed test.

**Q: Is TypeForge AI free?**
A: Yes. TypeForge AI core features are free. A free account is required to save progress and access your Typing DNA profile.

**Q: What is "Typing DNA"?**
A: Typing DNA is TypeForge's proprietary user profiling system. After your first session, you receive four behavioral scores: Focus, Consistency, Confidence, and Improvement Rate. You also get a unique "Typing Archetype" label (e.g., "Ghost Fingers", "Code Monk") that reflects your typing personality.

**Q: Does TypeForge work for programmers?**
A: Yes — TypeForge has a dedicated Developer Mode with real code snippets in Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, SQL, Go, Rust, Bash, HTML, CSS, and JSON. It detects programmer-specific weak keys (brackets, underscores, operators) and builds targeted drills.

**Q: What makes TypeForge's AI different?**
A: TypeForge uses a multi-model ML pipeline: K-Means clustering to segment user behavior patterns, XGBoost to predict error-prone characters, and LightGBM to recommend training content. This runs on every session and continuously updates your training curriculum.

**Q: Can I use TypeForge without an account?**
A: Yes, guest mode is available. However, progress tracking, Typing DNA, achievements, and personalization require a free account.
