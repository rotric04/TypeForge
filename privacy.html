Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 4, 2026

At TypeForge AI, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the keystroke data and personal information you share with us. This policy describes how we collect, store, verify, and secure your data.

1. Data Collection & Keystroke Telemetry

To provide adaptive typing feedback, we collect real-time typing dynamics (reaction speeds, correction times, keystroke delay sequences). This data is utilized solely by our python-based Machine Learning models to calculate consistency metrics, weak keys, and performance paths. We do not key-log personal credentials, passwords, or form entries.

2. Authentication & Profile Storage

User accounts and credential management are handled securely via Clerk. Clerk verifies logins, sessions, and multi-factor authorization tokens. User profiles, metrics history, and badge unlocks are saved directly inside our secure Supabase PostgreSQL database tables behind Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.

3. Third-Party Integrations

We work with trusted services to secure and optimize the platform:

  • Clerk: Profile management and secure JWT verification.
  • Supabase: Persistent PostgreSQL storage.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: Bot protection and challenge checks.

4. GDPR & Data Export Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you retain full ownership of your typing logs. You can request a full data export or delete your account permanently inside the Settings view.
